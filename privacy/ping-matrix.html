🏎️ PingMatrix Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 15, 2026

Zero Personal Data Collection: PingMatrix is a standalone, on-device gaming network diagnostic utility. It does not require user registration, does not collect personal identifiers, and only measures TCP/DNS round-trip times directly from your device to public gaming nodes.

1. Floating Overlay Permission (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

PingMatrix requests the SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW permission strictly to render the floating in-game HUD overlay on top of mobile games when requested by the user. The floating overlay does not capture screen contents, record keystrokes, or intercept gameplay.

2. Network & Latency Probes

PingMatrix performs short TCP connection probes to public gaming datacenters (Riot, PUBG, Fortnite, Valve, AWS, Cloudflare) solely to calculate ping (ms), jitter variance, and packet loss percentages.

3. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

PingMatrix uses Google AdMob to deliver rewarded video ads for unlocking extended floating HUD sessions. AdMob may collect standard non-identifying device metrics in compliance with Google's Privacy Policy.
