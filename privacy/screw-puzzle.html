Privacy Policy

Application: Wood Nuts & Bolts (com.foor.screwpuzzle)
Last Updated: October 10, 2026

Welcome to Wood Nuts & Bolts ("we", "our", or "us"), provided by LinuxCTRL. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information collected when you use our mobile puzzle game (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Local Device Storage

The App saves your puzzle progression locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences:

  • Current level progression, star ratings, and puzzle solutions.
  • In-game preferences including sound effects, music volume, and haptic feedback.

All game saves remain solely on your device and are never transmitted to external developer servers.

B. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App integrates Google AdMob (Google LLC) to deliver non-intrusive advertisements (banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads). Google AdMob may collect certain device diagnostics and advertising data automatically:

  • Device Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID) and hardware identifiers.
  • Coarse Location Data: General geographic information derived from your IP address.
  • Interaction Data: Ad impressions, click activity, and performance diagnostic logs.

For detailed information on how Google manages your data, please see the Google Privacy Policy & Partner Technologies.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Data collected by the App and Google AdMob is utilized strictly to:

  • Store and retrieve your puzzle progression locally.
  • Deliver, optimize, and monetize the game through Google AdMob.
  • Ensure security, combat ad fraud, and monitor application stability.

3. Device Permissions

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely to trigger haptic feedback sensations when unscrewing bolts, tapping holes, and dropping plates.
  • android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and display advertisements.

4. Managing and Opting Out of Targeted Ads

You have full control over personalized ads on your Android device:

  • Navigate to Settings > Google > Ads on your Android device to reset or delete your Advertising ID.
  • Visit Google Ad Settings to manage advertising preferences.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Wood Nuts & Bolts is a family-oriented casual physics puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Advertisements served in the App adhere to Google Play Families Policy standards.

6. User Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA regulations:

  • We do not collect, buy, or sell personal data.
  • You can erase your local puzzle saves and game data at any time by clearing application data in Android Settings > Apps > Wood Nuts & Bolts > Storage, or by uninstalling the application.
  • You can exercise opt-out rights for interest-based advertising directly through your Android device settings.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
