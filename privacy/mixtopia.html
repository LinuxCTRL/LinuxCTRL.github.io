Privacy Policy

Application: Mixtopia (com.foor.mixtopia)
Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Welcome to Mixtopia ("we", "our", or "us"), published by foor. Mixtopia is an offline puzzle and discovery game for Android. We respect your privacy and have designed the App so that your gameplay stays on your own device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and the choices available to you. It is written to align with Google Play Developer Policies, the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

🔒 Summary of Core Privacy Commitments:
• Mixtopia has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
• All game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.
• The game is fully playable offline and we do not operate servers that store your gameplay data.
• Free play is supported by Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos and occasional interstitials. There are no banner ads and no app-open ads.
• We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Mixtopia is built with a local-first architecture. The following data is saved only inside the App's private storage (a local SQLite database) on your device:

  • Game Progress: The elements you have discovered and the recipes you have already combined.
  • Hint State: Your remaining free hints and the timestamp used to regenerate them over time.
  • Preferences: Your selected language (English or Arabic), sound effects, haptic feedback, and theme settings.
  • Consent State: A local record of the advertising consent choice you made through Google's consent form.

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server operated by the developer. You can erase it at any time (see Section 6).

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Mixtopia free, the App integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google LLC. AdMob may automatically collect certain pseudonymous device data in order to deliver advertisements, measure their performance, and prevent fraud, in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.

A. Information Processed by AdMob

  • Device Identifiers: Your advertising ID (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID), as permitted by Android and your consent choices.
  • Coarse Location: A general region or country derived from your IP address. Mixtopia never requests precise GPS location.
  • Ad Interaction & Diagnostics: Which ads were loaded or shown, ad performance metrics, and crash or diagnostic data used to keep advertising stable and to detect invalid traffic.

B. Ad Formats Used in Mixtopia

  • Rewarded Video Ads: Completely voluntary, user-initiated videos. You choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game hint. The reward is granted only after the ad's reward event fires.
  • Interstitial Ads: Occasional full-screen ads shown only at natural breaks, such as after dismissing a "new element discovered" celebration. They are frequency-capped and are never shown during a drag, on first launch, or in your first session.

Mixtopia does not display banner ads or app-open ads.

For more information about how Google processes advertising data, and how to control personalized ads, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Ads Settings & Opt-Out Controls

3. Consent and Your Advertising Choices

Where required by law — including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland — Mixtopia uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before requesting personalized advertising. If you do not consent, the App requests non-personalized ads instead.

  • You can review or change your consent choice at any time from the App's privacy options in Settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or disable ad personalization, in your device settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Where required, the App also honors U.S. state privacy signals and choices through Google's consent tools.

4. Device Permissions Requested

Mixtopia requests only the permissions it needs to run:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Used solely to load advertisements through Google AdMob. All game content is bundled with the App, so the game itself works offline.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely for optional haptic feedback. It can be turned off in the App's Settings at any time.

Permissions we do not use: Mixtopia does not access your camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, SMS, phone state, calendar, or personal files. The App plays short sound effects but does not record audio.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. The only third party that processes data in connection with the App is Google AdMob and its advertising partners, which act as independent data controllers for advertising purposes. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

6. Data Retention, Security, and Deletion

Locally stored progress and settings remain on your device until you delete them. You can erase all local data at any time by:

  • Using Settings > Reset Progress inside the App, or
  • Clearing the App's storage from Android settings, or
  • Uninstalling the App.

We do not operate servers that store your gameplay data. Advertising data is retained by Google in accordance with Google's own policies. Data sent by the AdMob SDK is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Mixtopia is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personally identifiable information from children. Advertising is configured with a maximum ad content rating of "G" (general audiences), and personalized advertising is only requested where consent has been obtained. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. International Users

Mixtopia is available worldwide. Google may process advertising data in the United States and other countries where it operates, using safeguards described in the Google Privacy Policy. Where local law requires consent for personalized advertising, the App asks for it before serving such ads.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, its advertising partners, or applicable law. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
