Privacy Policy

Application: MAN OUT! Repair Workshop (com.manout.app)
Last Updated: October 19, 2026

Welcome to MAN OUT! Repair Workshop ("we", "our", or "us"), provided by LinuxCTRL. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information collected when you play our mobile action arcade game (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Local Device Storage (AsyncStorage)

The App saves your gameplay data strictly on your device using local storage:

  • Game Progression: Current shift level, cash earned, quota status, high score, and unlocked permanent hardware upgrades.
  • User Preferences: Sound FX settings, haptic vibration toggle, and selected visual workshop theme.

All game saves and settings remain exclusively on your device and are never uploaded or transmitted to external developer servers.

B. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App integrates Google AdMob (Google LLC) to deliver non-intrusive advertisements. Google AdMob may collect certain diagnostics and advertising metadata automatically:

  • Device Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID) / iOS IDFA and device specifications.
  • Coarse Location Data: General geographic information derived from your IP address.
  • Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, and diagnostic performance logs.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review the Google Privacy Policy & Partner Sites.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

The data processed by the App and third-party ad services is utilized strictly to:

  • Save and restore your in-game workshop progress and preferences locally.
  • Display and optimize in-game advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Detect, prevent, and combat technical fraud or instability.

3. Device Permissions

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback sensations during wrench repairs, fire extinguishing, and emergency alarms.
  • android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and display advertisements.

4. Managing and Opting Out of Targeted Ads

You maintain full control over personalized advertising on your mobile device:

  • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads to reset or delete your Advertising ID.
  • Google Account: Visit Google Ad Settings to adjust your ad personalization preferences.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

MAN OUT! Repair Workshop is a general audience, family-friendly mobile action game. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. All advertising served complies with Google Play Families Policy requirements.

6. User Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

In accordance with GDPR (European Union) and CCPA/CPRA (California) privacy frameworks:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • You can erase all locally stored data at any time via the in-game Settings menu ("Reset Game Progress") or by clearing application data in your device's Application Settings.
  • You can opt out of interest-based ad tracking through your device's system settings.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Updated policies will be posted directly to this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
