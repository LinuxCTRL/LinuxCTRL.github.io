Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 16, 2026

Welcome to Sift: Sand Puzzle ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed when you use our mobile application (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

A. Local Storage Data

The App saves your gameplay progress and preferences locally on your mobile device:

  • Unlocked level progression and completed sand artworks
  • In-game coin balance
  • App preferences (sound effects and haptic vibration toggles)

Note: All level progression, coins and settings remain strictly on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.

B. Automated Data Collection (Google AdMob)

The App integrates Google AdMob (Google LLC) to deliver banner ads, interstitial transition ads, and rewarded video advertisements for free in-game rewards. Google AdMob may automatically collect device details, such as:

  • Device Advertising ID (e.g., AAID / IDFA)
  • IP address and coarse location data
  • Ad performance metrics and crash diagnostics

To learn more about how Google handles user data, visit Google Privacy & Terms.

C. Consent (GDPR / EEA & UK)

Where required by law, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before serving personalized advertising. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time through the on-screen consent options or your device's ad settings.

2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used exclusively to:

  • Persist your level progression, coin rewards and preferences locally on your device
  • Serve banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements via Google AdMob
  • Maintain app security, stability and optimal performance

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information. The App relies on Google AdMob and its advertising partners, which act as independent data controllers for advertising purposes. Please review the following policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Data Retention & Security

Locally stored progress remains on your device until you clear the App's data, reset your progress from the Settings screen, or uninstall the App. We do not operate servers that store your personal gameplay data. Advertising data is retained by Google in accordance with its own policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Sift: Sand Puzzle does not knowingly collect or solicit personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • Reset all locally stored progress at any time from the in-app Settings → Reset progress option.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising by resetting your advertising ID or disabling ad personalization in your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data from your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at our developer portal.
