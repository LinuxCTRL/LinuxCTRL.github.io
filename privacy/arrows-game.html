Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2, 2026

Welcome to Arrows Game ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information collected when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Local Storage Data

The App stores game data locally on your device (e.g. via local device storage). This includes:

  • Game progress, unlocked levels, and star ratings
  • High scores and achievement progress
  • App settings (sound effects, haptic vibration, visual theme preference)

Note: This data remains exclusively on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

B. Automated Data Collection (AdMob)

The App integrates third-party ad services via Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect information automatically to serve ads, prevent fraud, and report ad performance, such as:

  • Device identifiers and Advertising ID (e.g., AAID)
  • IP address and coarse location data
  • App usage data related to ad interactions

Learn more about how Google uses data by visiting Google Privacy & Terms.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Save your level progress and game settings locally
  • Serve contextual banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads via Google AdMob
  • Maintain app stability and improve performance

3. Your Choices & Opt-Out

You can manage personalized advertising preferences in your mobile device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@yourdomain.com
