Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

Welcome to Pixel Paint 3D - Voxel Color Relaxation ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Local Storage Data

The App stores game progress and settings locally on your device via local AsyncStorage. This includes:

  • Colored 3D voxel model progress and completed artwork achievements
  • Unlocked voxel models and booster counts (Color Splash Bombs, Magic Wands)
  • App settings (haptic vibration and sound feedback)

Note: All artwork progress remains exclusively on your device and is not transmitted to external cloud servers.

B. Automated Third-Party Data Collection (AdMob)

The App integrates advertising services provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC) to serve interstitial and rewarded video ads for unlocking premium voxel models and booster power-ups. AdMob may collect data automatically, such as:

  • Device identifiers and Advertising ID (e.g., AAID)
  • IP address and coarse location data
  • App interaction data related to ad performance

Learn more about how Google handles user data by visiting Google Privacy & Terms.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Save your 3D voxel coloring progress and unlocked models locally
  • Serve rewarded video ads to unlock premium 3D voxel models and boosters
  • Maintain app stability and performance

3. Your Choices & Opt-Out

You can manage advertising and tracking preferences directly in your mobile device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@linuxctrl.github.io
