Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

Welcome to Word Search Jam — Unscramble & Crossword ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed when you play our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Local Storage Data

The App saves your gameplay progress, coin balance, and settings locally on your mobile device (via local storage):

  • Unlocked level progression (100 levels across 20 chapters) and star ratings
  • In-game coin balance, unlocked visual color themes, and bonus word jar counts
  • App preferences (sound effects and haptic vibration toggles)

Note: All level progression remains strictly on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

B. Automated Data Collection (Google AdMob)

The App integrates Google AdMob (Google LLC) to deliver banner ads, interstitial transition ads, and rewarded video advertisements for free in-game rewards. Google AdMob may automatically collect device details, such as:

  • Device Advertising ID (e.g., AAID / IDFA)
  • IP address and general geographic location
  • Ad performance data and crash diagnostics

To learn more about how Google handles user data, visit Google Privacy & Terms.

2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used exclusively to:

  • Persist your level progression, coin rewards, and visual themes locally
  • Serve contextual advertisements and rewarded ad unlocks via Google AdMob
  • Maintain app security, stability, and optimal performance

3. Children's Privacy

Word Search Jam does not knowingly collect or solicit personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at our developer portal.
