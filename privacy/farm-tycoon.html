Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 3, 2026

Welcome to Farm Tycoon: Cozy Idle Farm ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information collected when you play our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Local Device Storage (SQLite & Preferences)

The App stores game state and progression locally on your device using an embedded SQLite database and local device storage. This includes:

  • Farm plot layout, crop timers, and expansion progression
  • Animal state, production building queues, and inventory counts
  • Player coins, gems, level XP, and milestone achievements
  • App preferences (audio settings and notification choices)

Note: All farm data remains strictly on your device and is not uploaded to external web servers.

B. Automated Data Collection (Google AdMob)

The App integrates third-party ad services via Google AdMob (Google LLC) to deliver non-intrusive advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect device information such as:

  • Device identifiers and Advertising ID (e.g., AAID)
  • IP address and coarse location data
  • Ad view metrics, interactions, and performance diagnostics

Learn more about how Google uses data by visiting Google Privacy & Terms.

2. How We Use Information

We use information solely to:

  • Save your farm layout, inventory, and idle progress locally
  • Serve contextual banner and rewarded video ads via Google AdMob
  • Ensure smooth app performance, stability, and bug prevention

3. Your Choices & Ad Control

You can adjust or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking

4. Children's Privacy

Farm Tycoon: Cozy Idle Farm does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
