🌿 FloraDoc Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 15, 2026

100% On-Device AI Privacy: FloraDoc processes plant images and leaf scans directly on your device using on-device machine learning models. Your plant photos and garden data are never uploaded to any remote server.

1. Camera & Image Data

FloraDoc requires camera access exclusively to allow real-time leaf scanning and disease diagnosis. All photo processing, disease classification, and treatment lookups occur locally on your hardware. Images are never collected, transmitted, or sold.

2. Data Stored Locally on Device

The following data is saved exclusively on your local device storage:

  • Your saved garden plants and nicknames
  • Custom watering intervals and care schedules
  • Saved offline disease notes and treatment bookmarks

3. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

FloraDoc uses Google AdMob to deliver rewarded video ads. Google AdMob may collect standard non-identifying telemetry (such as advertising ID and general device specs) to serve relevant advertisements in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
