Privacy Policy for FOOR VPN

Last updated: August 15, 2026
Strict No-Logs Promise: FOOR VPN does not collect, monitor, store, or log any browsing activity, DNS queries, visited URLs, traffic destinations, or payload contents.

1. Overview

FOOR VPN ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy and security. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when using our Android mobile application.

2. Android VpnService & Network Routing

FOOR VPN utilizes Android's native VpnService API to establish a secure, encrypted virtual network interface (TUN) on your device. The primary function is to:

  • Encrypt network packets between your device and selected proxy server endpoints (VLESS, VMess, Trojan, SSH, SlowDNS).
  • Protect your traffic on public or untrusted Wi-Fi hotspots.
  • Route network traffic strictly according to your selected carrier tweaks and server settings.

We do NOT store or log any data transmitted through the tunnel.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No Browsing History: We do not log websites you visit or apps you use.
  • No Traffic Data or IP Logs: We do not record original IP addresses, connection timestamps, or session bandwidth logs.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not require registration, email addresses, names, or phone numbers to use the app.

4. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The app stores minimal operational preferences locally on your device:

  • Selected server and carrier tweak configuration.
  • Remaining connection session time (used for the countdown timer).
  • Custom user-imported or exported configuration files (encrypted with AES-256).

5. Advertising & Third-Party Services

To provide free access to high-speed servers, FOOR VPN uses third-party advertising partners (Google AdMob). When you interact with rewarded video ads to extend your connection time, Google Mobile Ads may collect non-personal device information according to their policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Data Disclosure

6. Security

We prioritize the security of your connections. Encrypted configs shared via the app are protected using industry-standard AES-256 cryptography.
