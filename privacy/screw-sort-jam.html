Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2, 2026

Welcome to Nut & Bolt — Unbolt Jam ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information collected when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Local Storage Data

The App stores game progress and settings locally on your device (via local storage):

  • Unlocked level progression, star ratings, and high scores
  • App preferences (sound effects and haptic vibration settings)

Note: All level progression remains exclusively on your device and is never sent to external servers.

B. Automated Data Collection (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob (Google LLC) to deliver banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements. Google AdMob may collect device information automatically, such as:

  • Device identifiers and Advertising ID (e.g. AAID / IDFA)
  • IP address and coarse location data
  • Ad interaction metrics and diagnostics

Learn more about how Google uses data by visiting Google Privacy & Terms.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Save your level progression and preferences locally
  • Serve contextual advertisements via Google AdMob
  • Maintain app security, performance, and stability

3. Children's Privacy

Nut & Bolt Unbolt Jam does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
