Privacy Policy for Hyper-Land

Effective Date: August 5, 2026

Welcome to Hyper-Land ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing a high-performance, offline-first developer learning platform.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require user registration or personal account creation to use Hyper-Land. Your learning progress, quiz scores, bookmarks, and theme preferences are stored strictly on your local device storage (via MMKV and local file system).

2. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

To support the development and maintenance of our app, Hyper-Land uses third-party advertising services provided by Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect and process pseudo-anonymous device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA) and standard network data to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements within the app.

For more information on how Google processes user data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Local Storage

Hyper-Land stores app configuration data, cached technical articles, quiz progress, and theme settings locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers and remains completely under your control.

4. Children's Privacy

Hyper-Land does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can immediately remove it.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.
