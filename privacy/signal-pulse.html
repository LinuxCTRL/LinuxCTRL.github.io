📡 SignalPulse Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 15, 2026

100% Local Diagnostics: SignalPulse analyzes Wi-Fi signals, frequencies, and channel spectrums locally on your device. Your Wi-Fi network passwords, SSIDs, and location heatmaps are never transmitted or stored on remote servers.

1. Location & Wi-Fi Permissions

Under Android OS specifications, Wi-Fi channel scanning and access point discovery require the ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION permission. SignalPulse uses this permission solely to discover local Wi-Fi frequencies and render your channel congestion spectrum graph. Your physical GPS coordinates are never recorded or transmitted.

2. Heatmap & Local Telemetry

All signal probes and custom room heatmaps created in the app remain strictly within your device's private local memory and can be reset at any time.

3. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

SignalPulse uses Google AdMob to deliver rewarded video ads for exporting detailed reports. AdMob may collect standard non-identifying telemetry to serve advertisements in compliance with Google's Privacy Policy.
