Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2, 2026

Welcome to Lady Recipes ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information collected when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Local Storage Data

The App stores data locally on your device (via AsyncStorage / local storage). This includes:

  • Saved favorite recipes and collections
  • Weekly meal planning calendar items
  • Consolidated grocery shopping checklists
  • App preferences and cached recipes for offline cooking mode

B. Community Reviews

When you voluntarily write and submit a recipe review, the information you provide (author display name, star rating, and review comment) is transmitted and stored in our database so other community members can benefit from your feedback. No email or password is required or collected for submitting reviews.

C. Automated Data Collection (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob (Google LLC) to deliver banner, interstitial, and video advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process device information automatically, such as:

  • Device identifiers and Advertising ID (e.g. Google Advertising ID / AAID)
  • IP address and coarse location data
  • Ad interaction metrics and diagnostics

For more details on how Google handles user data, visit Google Privacy & Terms.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Maintain your saved recipe bookmarks and app settings locally
  • Serve non-intrusive advertisements via Google AdMob
  • Ensure app security, performance, and stability

3. Children's Privacy

Lady Recipes does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

4. Your Choices & Opt-Out

You can opt out of personalized ad tracking via your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: foor.support@gmail.com
