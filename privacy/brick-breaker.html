Privacy Policy

Application: Neon Animal Brick Breaker (com.foor.brickbreaker)
Last Updated: October 10, 2026

Welcome to Neon Animal Brick Breaker ("we", "our", or "us"), provided by LinuxCTRL. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information collected when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Local Storage Data (Device Sandboxed)

The App stores your gameplay progression and preferences locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences:

  • High scores, level completion status, and unlocked animal brick stages.
  • In-game audio, music, and haptic vibration preferences.

This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded to private developer servers.

B. Third-Party Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

The App integrates Google AdMob (Google LLC) to display advertisements (banner and interstitial ads). To deliver and optimize ads, Google AdMob may collect and process certain information automatically, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) and unique device identifiers.
  • Network & Location Information: IP address and coarse/approximate geographic location.
  • App Usage & Diagnostics: Ad performance, user interaction events, and crash logs.

To learn more about how Google handles collected data, please review the Google Privacy Policy & Partner Technologies.

2. How We Use Collected Information

We and our third-party advertising partners use data solely to:

  • Save your in-game high scores, unlocks, and preferences locally on your phone.
  • Deliver and measure relevant advertising via Google AdMob.
  • Monitor application stability, prevent fraudulent ad clicks, and diagnose technical crashes.

3. Device Permissions Used by the App

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback when balls impact bricks and paddles.
  • android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by Google Play Services to load and display advertisements.

4. Opting Out of Targeted Advertising

You can manage or opt out of personalized advertisements at any time through your Android device settings:

  • Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.
  • Visit Google Ad Settings to manage your ad personalization preferences.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Neon Animal Brick Breaker is designed as a casual family-friendly arcade game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. All ads served through Google AdMob comply with Google Families Policy requirements.

6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

In accordance with GDPR (EU/UK) and CCPA/CPRA (California, USA):

  • We do not collect or sell your personal information directly.
  • You have the right to request deletion of your local game data by selecting Clear Data in your device Application Manager or uninstalling the App.
  • You can control third-party ad personalization directly via Android operating system privacy controls.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
