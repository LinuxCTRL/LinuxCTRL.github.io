Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

Welcome to 3D Polar Maze (Package: com.foor.polarmaze) developed by Foor / LinuxCTRL ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data processed when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Local Storage Data (On-Device Only)

The App stores your gameplay progression and personal preferences locally on your device via secure private storage:

  • Highest completed levels and 3-star maze ratings
  • Collected coin currency balance and unlocked 3D marble skins
  • Audio sound effects, haptic vibrations, and control settings (Touch Joystick vs Gyro Tilt)
  • Selected castle environment theme

Note: All gameplay progress and economy data remain strictly on your device and are never transmitted to external private servers.

B. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App integrates Google AdMob (Google LLC) to display advertisements (banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded video ads). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device information in accordance with its privacy policies, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Device model, operating system version, and screen metrics
  • IP address and general country/city level location
  • Ad impression, click, and interaction diagnostics

For more information on how Google processes data for advertising, please review How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by the App is used exclusively to:

  • Persist your game progress, stars, coins, and settings
  • Serve contextual and rewarded video ads to keep the game free to play
  • Ensure performance, 60 FPS rendering stability, and crash prevention

3. Children's Privacy

3D Polar Maze does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The game contains family-friendly puzzle gameplay. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly take appropriate action.

4. Your Privacy Rights & Ad Personalization Opt-Out

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising at any time directly through your Android device settings:

  • Android: Open Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID or Delete Advertising ID.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.
