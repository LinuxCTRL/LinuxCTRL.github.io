Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2, 2026

Welcome to Pairly: Memory Match ("we", "our", or "us"), published by foor. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data when you play our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Device-Local Game Data

Pairly is designed with an offline-first architecture. All gameplay progress and configuration stay strictly on your device:

  • Personal Best Records: Best completion times, Harmony Grades (S, A, B, C), and total games played per mode and difficulty tier.
  • Settings & Preferences: Chosen color theme, audio sound effects toggle, haptics preference, reduce motion setting, card emoji pack, and language preference (English or Arabic).

Note: This data is stored locally via an encrypted/isolated SQLite storage layer on your device and is never uploaded to external servers.

B. Automated Advertising & Diagnostics (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob (Google LLC) to deliver banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded power-up ads. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device information:

  • Device identifiers and Advertising ID (such as Android Advertising ID / AAID)
  • General IP address and coarse approximate location data
  • Ad interaction, performance metrics, and crash diagnostics

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms for Partner Sites.

2. Consent Management (UMP)

In regions requiring consent (such as the European Economic Area and UK under GDPR / ePrivacy directives), Pairly utilizes Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent SDK to present consent choices regarding personalized ads and cookie/identifier access before requesting ads.

3. Children's Privacy

Pairly: Memory Match is designed for a general audience (13+) and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13.

4. Data Retention and Security

Since we do not operate user accounts or cloud databases for personal records, we do not store your personal information on our own servers. Uninstalling or clearing the app storage on your device permanently resets your local best times and settings.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
